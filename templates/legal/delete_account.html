Delete your SafeNet account

SafeNet by Safezone Tech

You can ask for your SafeNet account and its data to be deleted. In the SafeNet app: More → Delete my account (businesses) or the ⋮ menu → Delete my account (Wi-Fi customers). Or use this form.

What is deleted: your login, profile, phone number and contact details, your voucher codes and Wi-Fi sessions, and (for a business) its sites, devices, packages, team and customers list.
What is kept: payment and payout records, as long as tax and financial laws require (usually five years), without being used for anything else.
When: within 30 days. We may contact you to check the account is yours before deleting it.

Futa akaunti yako ya SafeNet

SafeNet ya Safezone Tech

Unaweza kuomba akaunti yako ya SafeNet na taarifa zake zifutwe. Kwenye programu ya SafeNet: Zaidi → Futa akaunti yangu (biashara) au menyu ⋮ → Futa akaunti yangu (wateja wa Wi-Fi). Au tumia fomu hii.

Kinachofutwa: akaunti yako ya kuingia, wasifu, namba ya simu na mawasiliano, vocha zako na muda wa Wi-Fi, na (kwa biashara) maeneo yake, vifaa, vifurushi, timu na orodha ya wateja.
Kinachobaki: kumbukumbu za malipo, kwa muda sheria za kodi na fedha zinavyotaka (kwa kawaida miaka mitano), bila kutumika kwa jambo jingine.
Lini: ndani ya siku 30. Tunaweza kuwasiliana nawe kuhakikisha akaunti ni yako kabla ya kuifuta.
