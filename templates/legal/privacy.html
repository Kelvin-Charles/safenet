Privacy policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

SafeNet is a Wi-Fi billing service made by Safezone Tech in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. Wi-Fi businesses (hotels, hostels, shops, cafés) use it to sell internet with vouchers and mobile money, and their customers use it to buy Wi-Fi and see what they bought. This policy explains what the SafeNet website, the SafeNet app and the Wi-Fi login pages collect, why, who sees it and how to have it deleted. It follows Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022.

Two kinds of people use SafeNet

  • Businesses (owners and their team) sign in with a username and password to run their Wi-Fi business.
  • Wi-Fi customers buy internet from a business, on its Wi-Fi login page or in the app, and can sign in to the app with their phone number and an SMS code or a voucher code.

For a business's customers, the business decides why their data is used; SafeNet keeps and processes it for the business so the Wi-Fi works.

What we collect

From businesses: business name, username, email address, phone number and language; the places (sites) and network devices set up; packages, prices and offers; team members' names, emails and roles; how the business is paid (mobile-money number, Lipa Namba or bank account) and its withdrawals; expenses it records; its SafeNet plan and bills.

From Wi-Fi customers: phone number; what was bought (package, price, time, place) and the voucher code; the name the mobile-money network gives for the payer, when it gives one; the device's network (MAC) address and IP address on the Wi-Fi, login times and data used, so the right device gets online for the time paid; SMS messages SafeNet sends (voucher codes, receipts and, if the business sends them, offers, with a way to stop them).

In the app: the sign-in token, kept in the phone's secure storage, and the language and theme chosen. When the phone joins a Wi-Fi network, the app asks SafeNet whether it is a SafeNet business's Wi-Fi so it can offer to buy there; this uses the network address SafeNet sees, not the phone's location. The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking, and does not read contacts, photos, files or location.

Why we use it

  • To sell and deliver Wi-Fi: take payments, create voucher codes, let the right device online and end its time when it runs out.
  • To send voucher codes, receipts and alerts by SMS and email.
  • To show businesses their sales, customers, earnings and reports, and pay out their money.
  • To keep the service secure: stop fraud, misuse and free-riding, and fix problems.
  • To meet legal duties, such as keeping payment records.

Who sees it

  • The Wi-Fi business you buy from sees your phone number, purchases, codes and sessions at its places.
  • Payment providers (ClickPesa, Snippe and the mobile-money networks) process payments and payouts.
  • Our SMS and email providers deliver messages.
  • Network equipment services the business uses (such as TP-Link Omada and Ruijie Cloud) receive the device address and code needed to let it online.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while the account is used. Login sessions and logs are kept as long as needed to run and secure the service. Payment and payout records are kept as long as tax and financial laws require (usually at least five years), even after an account is deleted. Everything else is deleted when you ask, or when a business closes its account.

How we protect it

Everything travels over HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, and payment and device keys are encrypted. Each business sees only its own data, and access by our team is limited to what support needs.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your details: businesses in their Settings; customers by asking the business or us.
  • Stop offer messages: use the stop link in the SMS.
  • Delete your account and data: in the app (More → Delete my account, or the menu for Wi-Fi customers) or at {{ request.host_url }}delete-account.
  • Ask anything about your data, or complain: write to {{ contact_email }}. You may also complain to Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Commission.

Children

SafeNet is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will post any change here with a new date, and tell businesses by email when a change matters.

SafeNet ni huduma ya kuuza Wi-Fi iliyotengenezwa na Safezone Tech, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. Biashara za Wi-Fi (hoteli, hosteli, maduka, migahawa) huitumia kuuza intaneti kwa vocha na pesa za simu, na wateja wao huitumia kununua Wi-Fi na kuona walichonunua. Sera hii inaeleza tovuti ya SafeNet, programu ya SafeNet na kurasa za kuingia Wi-Fi zinakusanya nini, kwa nini, nani anaona, na jinsi ya kuomba vifutwe. Inafuata Sheria ya Ulinzi wa Taarifa Binafsi ya Tanzania, 2022.

Aina mbili za watumiaji

  • Biashara (wamiliki na timu yao) huingia kwa jina la mtumiaji na nenosiri kuendesha biashara yao ya Wi-Fi.
  • Wateja wa Wi-Fi hununua intaneti kwa biashara, kwenye ukurasa wake wa kuingia Wi-Fi au kwenye programu, na wanaweza kuingia kwenye programu kwa namba ya simu na nambari ya SMS au vocha.

Kwa wateja wa biashara, biashara ndiyo huamua taarifa zao zitumikeje; SafeNet huzihifadhi na kuzichakata kwa niaba ya biashara ili Wi-Fi ifanye kazi.

Tunachokusanya

Kutoka kwa biashara: jina la biashara, jina la mtumiaji, barua pepe, namba ya simu na lugha; maeneo na vifaa vya mtandao vilivyowekwa; vifurushi, bei na ofa; majina, barua pepe na nafasi za wanatimu; jinsi biashara inavyolipwa (namba ya simu, Lipa Namba au akaunti ya benki) na pesa ilizotoa; matumizi inayorekodi; kifurushi chake cha SafeNet na bili.

Kutoka kwa wateja wa Wi-Fi: namba ya simu; kilichonunuliwa (kifurushi, bei, muda, eneo) na nambari ya vocha; jina la mlipaji linalotolewa na mtandao wa simu, likitolewa; anwani ya kifaa (MAC) na anwani ya IP kwenye Wi-Fi, muda wa kuingia na data iliyotumika, ili kifaa sahihi kipate intaneti kwa muda uliolipiwa; SMS ambazo SafeNet hutuma (vocha, risiti na, biashara ikituma, ofa, pamoja na njia ya kuzisimamisha).

Kwenye programu: alama ya kuingia, inayohifadhiwa kwa usalama kwenye simu, na lugha na mwonekano uliochagua. Simu ikiunganishwa na Wi-Fi, programu huiuliza SafeNet kama ni Wi-Fi ya biashara ya SafeNet ili ikupe nafasi ya kununua hapo; hii hutumia anwani ya mtandao ambayo SafeNet inaiona, si mahali simu ilipo. Programu haina matangazo wala programu za kufuatilia za watu wengine, na haisomi anwani za watu, picha, mafaili wala mahali ulipo.

Kwa nini tunazitumia

  • Kuuza na kutoa Wi-Fi: kupokea malipo, kutengeneza vocha, kuruhusu kifaa sahihi na kukisimamisha muda ukiisha.
  • Kutuma vocha, risiti na taarifa kwa SMS na barua pepe.
  • Kuonyesha biashara mauzo, wateja, mapato na taarifa, na kuwalipa pesa zao.
  • Kulinda huduma: kuzuia udanganyifu na matumizi mabaya, na kurekebisha matatizo.
  • Kutimiza wajibu wa kisheria, kama kuhifadhi kumbukumbu za malipo.

Nani anaziona

  • Biashara ya Wi-Fi unayonunua kwake huona namba yako ya simu, ulichonunua, vocha na muda wako kwenye maeneo yake.
  • Watoa huduma za malipo (ClickPesa, Snippe na mitandao ya pesa za simu) huchakata malipo.
  • Watoa huduma wetu wa SMS na barua pepe hufikisha ujumbe.
  • Huduma za vifaa vya mtandao anazotumia mfanyabiashara (kama TP-Link Omada na Ruijie Cloud) hupokea anwani ya kifaa na vocha zinazohitajika kukiruhusu.
  • Mamlaka, pale tu sheria inapotaka.

Hatuuzi taarifa binafsi wala kuzitumia kwa matangazo.

Tunazihifadhi kwa muda gani

Taarifa za akaunti huhifadhiwa wakati akaunti inatumika. Kumbukumbu za kuingia huhifadhiwa kwa muda unaohitajika kuendesha na kulinda huduma. Kumbukumbu za malipo huhifadhiwa kwa muda sheria za kodi na fedha zinavyotaka (kwa kawaida angalau miaka mitano), hata baada ya akaunti kufutwa. Mengine yote hufutwa ukiomba, au biashara ikifunga akaunti yake.

Tunavyozilinda

Kila kitu husafiri kwa HTTPS. Manenosiri huhifadhiwa kwa njia salama isiyosomeka, na funguo za malipo na vifaa husimbwa. Kila biashara huona taarifa zake tu, na timu yetu huona kile tu kinachohitajika kwa msaada.

Haki na uchaguzi wako

  • Kuona na kurekebisha taarifa zako: biashara kwenye Mipangilio; wateja kwa kuiomba biashara au sisi.
  • Kusimamisha ujumbe wa ofa: tumia kiungo cha kusimamisha kwenye SMS.
  • Kufuta akaunti na taarifa zako: kwenye programu (Zaidi → Futa akaunti yangu, au menyu kwa wateja wa Wi-Fi) au kwenye {{ request.host_url }}delete-account.
  • Kuuliza chochote kuhusu taarifa zako au kulalamika: tuandikie {{ contact_email }}. Unaweza pia kulalamika kwa Tume ya Ulinzi wa Taarifa Binafsi ya Tanzania.

Watoto

SafeNet haikutengenezwa kwa watoto chini ya miaka 13 na hatukusanyi taarifa zao kwa makusudi. Ukidhani mtoto ametupa taarifa, tuandikie tuzifute.
