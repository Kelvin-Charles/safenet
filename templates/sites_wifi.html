2 Prepare the access point
  1. Plug it into your internet router (PoE adapter: PoE → access point, LAN → router). Wait 2 minutes.
  2. If it was used before, hold its Reset button for 10 seconds.
  3. Open its page from a phone or laptop on the same router (find it in your router's device list, e.g. https://192.168.1.33), log in with admin / admin, choose AP Mode, and skip any TP-Link cloud sign-in.
  4. In Controller Settings, turn on Cloud-Based Controller Management and enter {{ omada_host }}. Save.

How it works

SafeNet runs the Omada Controller for you. Your access points connect to SafeNet over the internet; you don't need an Omada account or any other box.

When a guest pays or enters a voucher, SafeNet lets their phone online for the time they bought, and cuts them off when you press Disconnect on the Live page.

Works with TP-Link Omada access points: EAP225, EAP225-Outdoor, EAP245, EAP610, EAP650 and other EAP models.
