SafeNet sells internet on your Wi-Fi: guests see your login page, buy a package with mobile money or type a voucher code, and get online for the time they paid for. You manage packages, prices, customers and earnings from the SafeNet dashboard.

Your Wi-Fi equipment only has to do one thing: keep guests off the internet until SafeNet says they have paid. There are four ways to do that. Choose the one that matches what you have.

Recommended TP-Link Omada access points EAP225, EAP610 and other Omada (EAP) models. Plug in and point them to SafeNet. Nothing else to buy. SafeNet gateway box A small Linux computer in front of any access point (TP-Link, Comfast, Tenda…). Every feature. MikroTik router Already have a MikroTik? Paste one script and it checks codes with SafeNet. Other routers (RADIUS) Routers and controllers that can use a RADIUS server for logins.
