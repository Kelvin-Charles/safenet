About NAS Devices

Network Access Server (NAS) devices are your network equipment (routers, switches, access points) that communicate with FreeRADIUS for authentication.

  • IP Address: The IP address of your network device
  • Secret: Shared secret for RADIUS communication (must match device configuration)
  • Vendor: Device manufacturer - determines which RADIUS attributes are supported
